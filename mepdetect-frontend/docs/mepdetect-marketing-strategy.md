# MEPdetect Marketing & Copywriting Strategy Guide

## Executive Summary
MEPdetect (formerly YOLOplan) is positioned as a premium AI-powered tool that saves MEP professionals 80-90% time on material takeoffs. The core value proposition centers on speed, accuracy, and workflow integration.

---

## Target Audience Personas

### Primary Personas:

**1. Tom the Electrical Estimator** (40-55 years old)
- Works for mid-sized electrical contractor
- Processes 20-50 plan sets monthly
- Pain: Manual counting takes 4-6 hours per job
- Goal: Bid more jobs without hiring more staff
- Decision criteria: ROI, accuracy, ease of use

**2. Sarah the MEP Firm Owner** (35-50 years old)
- Runs 5-20 person MEP contracting business
- Needs: Competitive edge, cost control, scalability
- Pain: Losing bids due to slow turnaround times
- Goal: Win more profitable projects
- Decision criteria: Time savings, customer service, integration

**3. Mike the Project Engineer** (28-40 years old)
- Works in construction/BIM coordination
- Tech-savvy, early adopter
- Pain: Manual symbol detection errors cause rework
- Goal: Streamline preconstruction workflow
- Decision criteria: Accuracy, BIM integration, API access

## Objection Handling

### Common Objections & Responses:

**"Will it work on my drawings?"**
→ "MEPdetect handles PDFs, scanned images, and digital CAD files—even messy, hand-marked-up plans. Try it free to see for yourself."

**"I have custom symbols specific to my company"**
→ "Professional plan includes custom training. Upload 20-50 examples and the AI learns your symbols in 1-3 hours."

**"What if the AI makes mistakes?"**
→ "Every detection is marked for quick visual verification. You stay in control while the AI handles the tedious counting."

**"We already have takeoff software"**
→ "MEPdetect complements your existing tools with Excel/CSV export. Think of it as your AI assistant that feeds data to your current workflow."

**"Is this replacing our estimators?"**
→ "No—it's empowering them. Your team focuses on strategy and pricing while AI handles the counting grunt work."

**"How secure is our data?"**
→ "TLS in transit, encrypted at rest, and full data ownership — we never share your files. Enterprise clients can deploy on-premise so drawings never leave their network. We are not SOC 2 audited today; if that is a procurement requirement, say so and we will tell you exactly where we stand." (Never claim an audit or certification we do not hold.)

**Free Tier**: 5 drawings/month
- Purpose: Low-friction trial, product-led growth
- Converts: Hobbyists to brand advocates, occasional users to free marketing

**Pro Tier**: $29/month (sweet spot)
- Purpose: Primary revenue driver for small-medium contractors

- ROI: Pays for itself saving 1-2 hours on single project

**Enterprise Tier**: Custom pricing
- Purpose: Capture large firms, handle custom requirements
- Starts: ~$500-1000/month for teams
- Includes: White-label, API, dedicated support, SLA

**Annual Discount**: 20% (2 months free)
- Purpose: Improve cash flow, reduce churn
- Incentive: $29/mo → $279/year ($23.25/mo effective)

## Brand Voice Guidelines

**Tone**: Professional yet approachable
**Style**: Clear, benefit-focused, jargon-free
**Personality**: Helpful expert, not salesy pusher

**Do's**:
- Use industry terminology correctly
- Speak to pain points directly
- Back claims with specific numbers
- Show, don't just tell (demos, screenshots)
- Be honest about limitations

**Don'ts**:
- Overpromise or use hype
- Use buzzwords without substance ("revolutionary," "game-changing")
- Criticize competitors directly
- Use technical jargon without explanation
- Sound condescending about manual methods

**Example Good Copy**:
"Most contractors spend 4-6 hours manually counting symbols for a typical project. MEPdetect does it in 60 seconds with 95%+ accuracy."

**Example Bad Copy**:
"MEPdetect is a revolutionary, game-changing, industry-disrupting AI platform that will completely transform how you work!"
