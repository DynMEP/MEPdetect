1. What the service does

MEPdetect detects and counts symbols on mechanical, electrical and plumbing drawings, and on vector PDFs it traces conductor runs and produces a circuit netlist. It returns counts, coordinates, confidence scores, an annotated copy of the sheet, exports in Excel, CSV and JSON, and where tracing ran, a netlist and a traced overlay.

Detection is performed by a machine-learning model. It is probabilistic: it will miss symbols and it will report symbols that are not there, at a rate that depends on your drawings and on the model in use.
